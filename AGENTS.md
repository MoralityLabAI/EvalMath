# Instructions for agents working in EvalMath

Before proposing, training, scoring or interpreting a small or repeated evaluation, read [`skills/evalmath-mechinterp-power/SKILL.md`](skills/evalmath-mechinterp-power/SKILL.md) and run its preflight planner on independent development-root summaries. In particular, AF/PEF/SMC/LS-SAE/BPD/Hypergames evaluations with 64 individual outcomes require root-level accounting, a meaningful causal variation threshold, sham calibration and a sealed confirmation set.

For existing proofs and evidence labeling, follow `CLAUDE.md` and `REVIEW.md`. Preserve exact-rational receipt provenance, distinguish synthetic mechanisms from neural causality and avoid opening protected splits. Use `spiral_ln.evaluation_finite_sample` for paired and clustered inference after a protocol is frozen.
