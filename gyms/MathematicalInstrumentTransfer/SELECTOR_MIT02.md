## Exact feature aliasing witness

Stress seed 9001: weights (1,1,2,3,2,3,3,2,1,2,2,1); four masks (900,3605,1123,4092); costs (1,1,1,1); initial state (4095,15,4). Query indices 1 and 2 share all 11 scalar features, including split masses 13/23 and 10/23 and one-query success 15/23. Their exact continuation success masses are 19 and 18 out of 23. Query 1 is uniquely optimal at this state. The shared pointwise ranker assigns the two actions identical scores for every choice of weights. Fixed index tie-breaking can pick the optimal action in this example; relabeling the two actions preserves scalar features and removes any guarantee of choosing the better action. This is an expressivity witness for the feature map, not an empirical generalization result.
