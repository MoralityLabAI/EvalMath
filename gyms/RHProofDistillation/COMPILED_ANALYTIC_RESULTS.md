## Limits

The graph proves **syntactic occurrence of constants in Lean's original
compiled proof expressions**. It does not establish their minimal
mathematical necessity. The analytic excerpt is far smaller than the
66,168-module Galois-row import closure and the 175,184-module full
`Main` closure.

The `core_gm` two-hop expansion was truncated by its 120-node limit.
The direct root references above were captured in full; deeper nodes
need a wider bounded probe. The separate mathematical validity of
the global argument is outside this targeted extraction.
