Reviewing EvalMath

This guide is for a reviewer, human or model, asked to check this repository. The work was produced by a lead model delegating to subagents. Every delegated result was rerun and, for the sharpest claims, checked with independent code; the journey map and the redacted session log show how. That process found many errors, so assume more remain. The most useful review finds them.

What to check

  1. Every claim labelled algebraic identity. Is it proved for every parameter its statement covers, including boundary cases? Earlier errors clustered at boundaries: categories with no failures, accuracy exactly 1, horizons of one step, product indices starting at zero, ties between optimal actions.
  2. Every claim labelled finite verified example. Is it described as finite everywhere it is used, including in the synthesis? Is any grid regularity promoted to a theorem anywhere?
  3. The Lean formalizations. For each row of lean/CLAIMS.md, does the Lean statement say what the note says, with the same quantifiers and hypotheses? A row labelled full that is weaker than the note is a bug, and so is a hypothesis that makes the theorem trivial.
  4. The computer-assisted proof in paper/selection_law_kl_containment.md D.5 (K = 4 to 30). Check the constants' derivation, the directed rounding, and the two cited inputs (Robbins' Stirling bounds; Poisson summation, cited from memory).
  5. The counterexamples. Each is stated exactly; re-derive at least the gate counterexample in paper/evaluation_feedback_multigen.md §6 and Counterexample 9 in paper/leak_topology.md with your own code.
  6. The receipts. python scripts/check_receipts.py --all should report every receipt as regenerating identically apart from its timestamp, with matching source hashes.
  7. The synthesis. Does paper/feedback_channel_findings.md state each result no more strongly than its note does?

Results to test first

  • Training-signal L4: observed accuracy a − ε + 2(1 − a)ε₁ reaches the ceiling without memorization iff ε₁ ≥ ½, for a < 1.
  • Reuse inflation: the running maximum of GK + 1 draws and the bound √(ln(GK + 1)/(2n)).
  • Theorem B: KL(best-of-K ‖ base) ≤ ln K − (K − 1)/K for every finite law and tie rule.
  • Lattice Theorems 10 and 11 and Corollary 14 (committing until a hit is optimal iff H ≤ 2).
  • Multigen Proposition 10 and the two-gain counterexample.
  • Gold calibration: the six four-rater equalities, the copying-grader residual, and the coverage of the §11 interval.
  • Leak topology Theorem 1 (fibre dimension equals the cycle rank) and Counterexample 9.

How to report

For a wrong claim, give the note, the claim's label and an exact witness: parameters, state, and the values on each side. For a gap in a proof, name the step and what it assumes. For a Lean fidelity problem, give the theorem name and the hypothesis or quantifier that differs. Please keep findings separate from suggestions.
