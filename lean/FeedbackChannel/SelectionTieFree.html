 5# The tie-free value of Theorem B
 6
 7Source: `paper/selection_law_kl_containment.md`, draft 0.3, §2, proof step (ii): in the tie-free
 8(continuous) case the best-of-`K` law has density `K u^{K-1}` against the probability-integral
 9transform `u`, and its KL divergence is
10`∫₀¹ K u^{K-1} ln(K u^{K-1}) du = ln K - (K - 1)/K = λ_K`.
11
12`tieFree_integral` proves this integral identity (by the fundamental theorem of calculus with the
13antiderivative `G` of `SelectionContainment`). It is the integral that the discrete proof of
14Theorem B telescopes to; the measure-theoretic statement that an atomless score law attains it
15(the density computation of step (ii)) is not formalized.
16-/
