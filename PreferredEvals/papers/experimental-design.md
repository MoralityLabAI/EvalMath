# Experiment selection and execution

- [TasteVal (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06824): separate Researcher experimental choices from fixed Coder execution.
- [OpenAI PaperBench (2025)](https://openai.com/index/paperbench/): replicate 20 research papers through author-developed hierarchical grading. Check the 8,316 rubric fragments' dependence within paper roots.
- [OpenAI MLE-bench (2024)](https://openai.com/index/mle-bench/): 75 Kaggle engineering competitions, with fixed compute and human leaderboard references.
- [METR Q4 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-20-q4-2026-capability-update/): AI R&D time horizons and why long-feedback research judgment can be hard to verify.

**Design move:** lock research project, tool budget and grader; log experimental hypotheses, cost and failed attempts. Use repeated trials within root for uncertainty; independent projects provide the inferential unit.
