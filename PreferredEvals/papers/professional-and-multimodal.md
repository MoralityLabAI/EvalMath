# Professional work and multimodal generalization

- [OpenAI GDPval (2025)](https://openai.com/index/gdpval/): representative professional artifacts across 44 occupations, with expert review and 220 open gold cases.
- [Thinking Machines Inkling Verify](https://github.com/thinking-machines-lab/inkling-verify): MMMU-Pro and MMAU evaluation scripts; preserve media and tool transforms.
- [Inkling Model Card](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/inkling/): CharXiv, MMMU-Pro and audio reporting.
- [OpenAI HealthBench](https://github.com/openai/simple-evals): domain-expert rubric example; high-stakes outputs require stricter review and contextual limitations.

**Design move:** score entire artifacts, use blinded reviewers with agreements, stratify by occupation and modality, never use cross-domain averages to hide failures in one category.
