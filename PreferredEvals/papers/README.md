## General methodology

- [Anthropic, Demystifying Evals for AI Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) (2026): stable task execution, different grader families and the distinction between reliability and capacity.
- [EvalMath, A Calculus of Evaluation Design](../../paper/evaluation_design_calculus.md): \(G,\Pi,D,Z,J,\mathcal A,C\) across gaming, controller, acquisition and claim.
- [EvalMath, Finite Sample Inference](../../paper/finite_sample_inference.md): root-level samples, multiplicity, paired tests and evidence classes.
