# BPD-01 / BPD-LG-01: adversarial-review corrections (2026-11-15)

## Evidence disposition

BPD-01/T1 and T2 are valid finite decision-theoretic identities under a declared deterministic observation table. The minimum-weight pair cover and minimax adaptive decision tree are established formulations, not proofs of learned neural decomposition. T3 is the weighted majority-error identity for finite feature fibres. Continuous hidden states generally produce singleton fibres, giving a vacuous zero floor without a separately justified matching protocol. T4 is a synthetic refresh counterexample; the original `refill_witness` tabulates trajectories rather than executing an independently specified recurrent transition. A real cue-conflict intervention is needed.

**BPD-LG-01 is retained as a failed mechanism-separation benchmark.** It kernel-checked 192 synthetic positive proof scripts, but the six roots within each family/depth differed only by renamed binders. Its critical-promise deletion controlled the labels, the nuisance was an unused Nat binder, and some tactic scripts did not change with depth. Therefore the train/dev/test assignment did not protect against alpha-renamed statement reuse. Kernel validity certifies a proposition/proof pair; it does not certify a generalization split or causal mechanism distinction.

## Terminology and scope

Goodfire's method is **adVersarial Parameter Decomposition (VPD)** ([Goodfire paper](https://www.goodfire.com/research/interpreting-lm-parameters)), correcting the earlier informal expansion “Vector Parameter Decomposition.”

A finite table of mechanism hypotheses H is a **closed-world identifiability interface**. For an unknown mechanism outside H, an experiment can return an observation incompatible with every member, but a zero-error open-set guarantee needs an explicit alternative class and its observation kernels. Lookup-table explanations satisfy finite behavioural fidelity; interpretable decomposition requires a separate simplicity/minimality prior and consistent parameter/activation coordinates.

For hypothesis-dependent routing, route selection belongs in the observation alphabet when the routing result is observed. When missingness is latent, model the missingness mechanism and its selection bias. In a stochastic setting, discrimination is controlled by KL divergences across joint route/output distributions, under stated absolute continuity and sequential sampling assumptions. The binary-bandit QI-02 bound addresses two specified worlds.

The exact finite T3 fibre floor is a **diagnostic of an observation interface**, not a learned-state metric. For continuous recurrent states, preregister a tolerance sweep, held-out predictive accuracy, intervention labels at the next readout, and matched remaining iteration budgets. A source/carry cue-conflict swap is needed to test reconstruction versus persistence: perturb the carried component with source fixed, then source with state fixed, and jointly cut candidate backup paths.

The FC-12 Hall deficit concerns feasibility of assigning **verified positive** tactic labels under quotas. BPD-01 pair cover concerns **distinguishing declared mechanisms**. Of 204 training state-family cells, 39 have positive compiler evidence and 165 remain unknown; none of the untested cells is a verified failure, and the 117 proposed critical edges have no current success probability estimate.

## Successor and acceptance gates

The source-first BPD-LG-02 corpus lives at `MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/experiments/bpd_lean_curriculum_v2` and is independently mirrored on this branch for hosted Lean kernel replay. It uses distinct role-labelled premise graphs, interior-link counterfactuals, active type-correct decoys, 96 structural groups, balanced labels, and a separate model-visible record. Role-graph fingerprints ignore variable names but do not certify arbitrary semantic or graph-isomorphism uniqueness. The four tactic families still share a narrow synthetic logic grammar.

Future BPD mechanism claims require held-out **intervention** predictions on an unseen tactic family, a strong symbolic/taxonomy control, matched unrolled VPD and rotation nulls, joint-ablation prediction from single edits, and recurrence across decomposition seeds. Reporting the positive/negative Lean kernel results is a separate gate from measuring any neural capability or mechanistic gain.
