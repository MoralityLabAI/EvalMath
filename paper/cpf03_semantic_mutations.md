# CPF-03: statement-mutation curriculum

Research fixture for Data Goblin and proof-fidelity critics. No model evaluations, calibration, upstream kernel audit or estimate of real proof failure probability. 42 deterministic prompts in seven mechanism families, 18 train, six dev and 18 held-out test. The split is public and family-disjoint, not a sealed independent benchmark. Do not train on test answers and then claim generalization. With three held-out families it is too small to estimate population accuracy. Treat family, not paraphrase, as the independent unit.

The model receives only an ID and prompt (split is evaluator metadata). Source prompts include the relevant evidence packet, so they need no hidden retrieval context. Return a label and mathematical justification. `entailed` means established under the stated premises or at the stated interface evidence level; `counterexample` means a concrete witness violates the proposed statement or implication within its declared domain; `unresolved` means the cited interfaces do not establish the claim. Abstract countermodels establish logical nonimplication only, never an actual complexity-class separation. The gold export contains mechanism and evidence level; never expose those to a scored model. Labels are generated from stated rules; gate, uniformity and source cases have hand-written derivations and require independent human review before a sealed benchmark. The degree and field-polynomial cases have exact finite checks. The approximation rule checks exhaust all one-witness Boolean models; any failed universal inclusion implication has a one-witness countermodel, and valid pointwise implications extend to every universe. Lean candidates independently encode one inclusion proof and one converse countermodel plus the two threshold lemmas. These are not an upstream Lean proof audit.

The gate examples rely on CPF-02's simulation inequality, kernel-checked as `charged_free_sandwich`. A variable has zero free-gate cost and one charged-input gate. Repeated squaring of x yields x^(2^n) over every field, so small circuit cost alone does not give a polynomial degree bound. The concrete prompts compare exact degrees to explicit caps. In characteristic two, the determinant expansion and permanent expansion coincide because all signs become one; in odd characteristic the 2-by-2 all-ones matrix separates them. This does not assert a new asymptotic circuit bound. The finite-field cases separate coefficient equality from equality of evaluation functions. The pointwise threshold countermodel R(i,n) iff i<=n makes the quantifier-order issue explicit. The source cases concern the pinned CPF-02 comparator, scope note, statement and solution files, not the truth of the headline mathematical conjecture. Their gold reasons should be audited against the actual archived files.

Score exact label accuracy, separately score explanation/certificate fidelity with a deterministic witness checker where available, and report each family and evidence level separately. Abstaining on unsupported claims is intentional. An evaluation harness should require the model to justify its label, with no label/rule or gold source in the prompt; aggregate label accuracy is insufficient to establish mathematical critique ability. This fixture cannot estimate CPF-01 correlation: for that, preregister many independently sampled intended claims, two verification pipelines, gold evidence, and audit budget before either system sees labels.

Run `PYTHONPATH=src pytest -q tests/test_evaluation_cpf03.py` and `PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/verify_evaluation_cpf03.py --output output/evaluation_cpf/cpf_03.json --export-dir output/evaluation_cpf/cpf03`. The receipt verifies generation and abstract-oracle invariants and hashes the exports and source files; it does not independently adjudicate all handwritten gold reasons. The four Lean witnesses (`approximate_bound_transfer`, `no_reverse_bound`, `pointwise_threshold`, `no_uniform_threshold`) are kernel-checked with standard axioms only.
