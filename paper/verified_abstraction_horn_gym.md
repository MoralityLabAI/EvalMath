# VAD-06A: Horn-clause abstraction discovery

Status: 2026-11-25. Exact finite acyclic Horn model; generated Lean proof batches kernel-replayed. Evidence is restricted to the declared graph generator and reference-cost model.

## Mathematics

For facts F and a topologically ordered finite rule DAG with rules (A -> v), assign each fact and available verified cache reference cost 1. Define D(v) as the minimum of 1 when v is a fact or a cached atom and 1 + sum_{u in A} D(u) over rules concluding v, with infinity for unreachable atoms. Induction in topological order proves that D is the minimum number of references in a proof tree in this grammar: each non-leaf root must use a rule concluding v, and its children may independently attain the inductive minima. This is a proof-tree cost, not an optimum over all possible proof DAGs, arbitrary Lean terms, or unrestricted theorem-proving tactics.

For one cached intermediate a, the charged batch cost is D_empty(a) + sum_{g in G} D_{a}(g) + |G|-1 + S, where S is selection work and the conjunction of goals uses |G|-1 constructors. With no cache the construction term is zero. The cache is constructed from the original facts and rules in a local let binding, so its construction is charged once per batch. The conclusion is a conjunction of all target propositions. Fresh isolated files import Init only. Local caching across one joint proof under common hypotheses is different from transferring a globally quantified lemma between unrelated theorem contexts.

For support screening, propagate one bit per development goal backward from the goal through every rule concluding a reached atom, including alternatives. For each atom the resulting bitset is precisely its ancestor-cone membership across development goals, by reverse topological induction. This measures structural opportunity for reuse; membership in a cone does not establish that an optimal proof uses the atom or that deployment will benefit. Retain reachable non-fact intermediates with support at least two. Rank by projected horizon times support count times (D_empty(a)-1), less development-count times construction cost. Abstain if no positive candidate. The support_exact arm reranks this screened pool by exact development savings. Frequency counts occurrences on selected shortest proof trees; top4 reranks the four highest frequency candidates; exhaustive reranks the full development candidate menu; oracle uses deployment costs and is explicitly privileged. All non-oracle selectors use only the development cone for scoring. Target IDs are excluded from the menu; the generator also enforces that targets are terminal atoms, so no held target is an ancestor of a development goal. This is a synthetic scope guarantee, not a general dependency audit for arbitrary Lean libraries.

## Experiment

Use 48 public seeded families (67000 through 67047) with eight development goals and 256 distinct deployment goals each. Four regimes, 12 families per regime: shared hub, shifted hub, mixture of two hubs, and null private chains. Each target may have a private derivation and a competing route via a hub. Seven policies, four nested horizons (8, 32, 128, 256): 1,344 records. This is a fresh seed range after the exploratory 32-family run; no third-party sealed manifest. All construction and deployment costs are exact integer reference counts. Selection charges visited nodes, rule/argument scans, scoring and declared ranking work; the graph/index build, machine-level bitset costs, inference and physical kernel costs are outside this abstract model. The sensitivity metric also includes deployment dynamic-programming scans, using the common legal premise cone for comparable arms.

At horizon 256, reference cost without caching is 139,811 units. Frequency costs 142,470 (0.981336x), exhaustive 458,581 (0.304877x), support 133,164 (1.049916x), support_exact 164,793 (0.848404x). Support reduces total cost by 6,647 units (4.75%); it selects a cache in 36/48 families, with net savings in every shared and mixed family (24/24). Shared/mixed/shifted/null gains are 1.724960/1.123169/0.813908/0.900147. All 12 null families abstain but still pay search work; all 12 shifted families select and lose money. Including deployment scans lowers pooled support gain to 1.018863x. This is a positive finite mechanism result and a negative transfer control, not evidence of neural superiority.
