The projection includes encounter, turn, status and legal action IDs/text; no pre-root history, encounter or reaction prose. The belief-policy recursion maximizes integer utility mass over each observation cell. ## 4. Implications for authoring and evaluation

The largest improvement here comes from changing the reachable distribution via legal existing choices. This complements AC02's fixed-root coefficient obstruction. It exposes a design tradeoff: a simple four-site fixed strategy can make the secret ending attainable in over 90% of fresh public campaigns, while unique-truce frequency under that strategy is only 21/192. The all-investigate policy has more unique truce opportunities (56/192) with lower attainable utility. A high goal oracle alone does not establish balanced narrative difficulty. SRG-13's proposed target opportunity rate was 12.5–20% under its declared random-campaign protocol, so the optimized policy's distribution is different. It would be incorrect to promote the original candidate as a balanced authored world using the new distribution.

The current finite grammar proves only its searched policy results and restricted clairvoyant envelopes. Public seeds, small matched tape cohorts and empirical suffix lookup limit generalization. The 6.25% paired-loss budget is an experimental choice requiring author review; no author-approved tradeoff or narrative warrant is assumed. Source semantics, hard gate and utility remain unchanged. The actual production storyworld/editor, human or model performance, and independent private validation remain untested. Next authoring task: certify opportunity calibration under several player policies, detect fixed-script shortcuts, and preserve reachable evidence while creating temporal contrasts that require context-sensitive decisions. Do not optimize mere success rate or evidence mass as a universal authoring score.
