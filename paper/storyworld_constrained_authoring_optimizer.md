## 1. Contract and exact observation oracle

The candidate's three truce reactions write the same bounded coordination readiness r=0.125 min(1,64(ab+ac+bc)), where a,b,c are the existing timeline, radio and review records. Search r_w=0.125 min(1,64(w_ab ab+w_ac ac+w_bc bc)) over the 19 points w in {0.5,0.75,1,1.25,1.5}^3 with sum w=3. Other formulas, gates, route links, prose and coordinate declarations remain byte-identical. Unit weights reproduce the source exactly. Monotonicity in each nonnegative record and zero readiness with fewer than two nonzero records are preserved. The author must approve even this bounded redistribution; the existing records quantify investigative work and requests, not verified facts.

For a uniform finite empirical prior over reached first-sirens states with multiplicities, build all legal action trees to H=3 and compute an observation-history policy by recursively grouping successor states by the fixed projected observations after each action. Use either menu plus three exact records, or the same plus 4-bit dyadic bins for suspicion, escalation and loyalty. The projection includes encounter, turn, status and legal action IDs/text; it excludes earlier prefix history, encounter prose and reaction prose. This is an explicitly defined restricted channel; it is not a claim about everything a production player sees. Each group chooses the action maximizing the summed optimal continuation values over its next observation groups. Terminal truce payoff one, other terminals or unfinished horizon zero. Full-state ceiling optimizes per root. Informative root counts use full-state action vectors; off-policy observation-cell counts are separate diagnostic statistics. The exact empirical prior does not establish population performance or actual model reasoning. No independent belief-policy brute verifier is included in this stage.

Hard constraints from the registration: preserve root menus and nontruce selected successors; preserve the truce reaction identity, all noncoordination numerical fields, turn and spools; no loss of an already attainable H3 goal; no loss of either empirical observation-policy numerator or informative-root count; any newly gained truce H3 value requires two records; readiness <=.125 and added priority <=.5; selected truce step energy ratio <=1.25; maximum prefix-plus-step energy share increase <=.025. The geometric metric uses raw unit weights and is a local structural safeguard, not a universal narrative quality score. The source patch deliberately cannot introduce new evidence, route gates, or effects. Validation checks exact prefix replay equivalence.

## 4. Post-development continuous obstruction

On the development roots, 103/128 have zero readiness from fewer than two positive records. Three additional roots are constant at the cap throughout the weight grid, so only 22/128 have any readiness variation within the allowed polytope.

The uncapped weighted sum is linear on the continuous polytope [0.5,1.5]^3 intersected with sum w=3. Its vertices are the six permutations of (0.5,1,1.5), included in the grid. Capping at one preserves the extrema. Inspect the compiled AST: readiness is read only in the secret ending's priority, with positive slope 4. It is absent from other eligibility gates, scores, menu predicates, priorities and effects. Every truce reaction routes through the existing wild resolver. On a fixed reached root, the selected reaction and all other numerical fields are independent of w. Eligibility and other wild rankings are fixed; raising secret priority cannot convert an already selected secret ending into a different ending. The rootwise best immediate goal therefore occurs at a maximizing vertex, even if each root is allowed its own weight vector.
