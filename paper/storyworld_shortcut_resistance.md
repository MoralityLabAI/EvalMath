## 1. Policy class and information contract

Use the AC03 default, all-investigate (default,0,2,2) and frozen (0,0,1,2) upstream policies. At all other visits, use the same 24-word absolute-index random coupling. The calibration cohort has 96 tapes (seed 0x53522401), and two fresh cohorts have 128 tapes each (0x53522411/12). All 1056 policy prefixes reach first sirens, with no unavailable overrides or runtime failure. Generate all legal H=3 suffix trees with the actual SWMD runtime. Binary reward is reaching the secret ending within the suffix horizon. Full-state oracle maximizes per root. In the three upstream arms, this is a comparison of distributions induced by legal policies, not fixed latent roots across arms.

The restricted projection includes encounter, turn, status and legal action IDs/text; then optionally exact timeline/radio/review records; then b-bit dyadic bins for suspicion, escalation and loyalty (b=1,2,4,8); or all declared public coordinates. No prefix history, encounter prose or reaction prose is included. The ordered projected action-observation history is retained in the suffix. The menu is free side information; exact ledger values are not a bit-budget claim. For each projected history cell, exact integer-mass Bellman recursion evaluates every available action and conditions future choices on projected observations. For remaining horizon h, the d-lookahead control maximizes terminal reward on min(d,h) moves, then repeats the calculation at the next cell. It is fitted against the same empirical roots but scored by full H=3 rollout. d=3 is the exact empirical permitted-information oracle. d=1,2 are specified policy controls, not lower bounds on algorithmic reasoning depth. Verify nested d=3 ceilings, and exact self-rollout of fitted d=3 policies. Receding-policy utility need not be monotone with richer channels or d. Fit all policies on calibration, freeze their history-key lookup tables, and deploy on the evaluation roots with calibration menu fallback then first legal fallback. Within-cohort fitted policies are ceilings or diagnostic controls, not evidence of transfer.

Class A is the 64 deterministic rank sequences of length three, using zero-based ranks 0..3 and first legal when a rank is absent. Class B is a deterministic encounter-to-rank map using the maximum legal menu width observed at each reached page; absent rank or unseen page falls back to first legal. Exhaustively enumerate both classes, choosing ties in enumeration order. The calibration class B search uses only calibration encounter pages. Each observed map space was between 432 and 5184, below the registered cap of 262144, so all nine fits resolve exactly. These classes restrict context but still receive the legal menu; a sequence can adapt through which options are present. Report actual rollout successes, root informative accuracy, first-action and total regret. Strict reversal pairs are roots with different unique optimal first actions under H3, grouped by page or identical projection. These counts share roots and are not independent observations.

No other singleton ledger coordinate suffices; the empty-subset controls fail the 95% criterion. Therefore the minimum number of added ledger coordinates among the tested subset class is one. This is a task- and policy-distribution-specific information certificate, not a statement that the full storyworld has one latent bit, that the player can discard its menu/history, or that no other public variable could substitute for review. The subset choice was investigated after seeing the registered result; selection across 16 channels is public and its sufficiency needs independently frozen replication. The bit can be a proxy for route history, not a proved causal mediator. The calibration table has no model training and no test-root gold in fitting.

For authoring, a high-order coordination formula can collapse to a low-order effective decision rule under a strong upstream policy. The active reachable distribution is critical. The next hardening exercise should preserve legitimate access and provenance while constructing matched opposite-optimum histories with identical review presence, route, menu and time, varying independently warranted evidence. Then measure the observation-constrained optimum and fixed/context-blind policy gap on a fresh matched cohort. A harder goal should retain attainable opportunities and avoid unannounced new information.
