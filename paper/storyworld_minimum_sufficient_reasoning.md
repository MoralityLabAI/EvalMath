This measures the declared information channel, not the actual production UI.

For a belief cell B of latent nodes reached under a shared observed history, V_h(B) = max_a sum_o V_(h-1)({next(s,a):s in B, O_S(next(s,a))=o}); terminal reward contributes one for the chosen goal and zero otherwise. Sum over initial observation cells. Let F be full-state attainable utility mass, P(S) the empirical permitted-information optimum, and R(S) the ceiling allowing privileged continuation after a shared first action. Then P(S)<=R(S)<=F. Projection refinement gives monotonicity for both. Report min |S| attaining F, the all-six value, and ceil(.95 F), including all inclusion-minimal subsets. The difference F-R(S) is first-action ambiguity, while R(S)-P(S) is additional continuation ambiguity. These are finite empirical utility quantities, not population Bayes values or computational time lower bounds.

A separate root-only certificate uses unique-optimum attainable roots in each free-menu cell. For different unique action labels, let D be the mask of differing coordinate values. S separates this pair iff S intersects D. The family of nonzero masks is a finite hitting-set problem. D=0 is an unseparable conflict. Verify all 64 candidate masks by comparing the predicted collision count with direct projected grouping; the inclusion-minimal masks hitting every nonzero conflict need not solve full-policy utility because ties, nonunique winner sets and downstream information can matter. This distinguishes causal interaction order from observational sufficiency on a particular distribution. A proxy may separate outcomes without being a causal mediator.

## 5. Controlled SWMD K ladder

To separate ambient dimensions from required information, construct actual SWMD-SRG/2 fixtures for J in {1,2,4,6} and K in {8,32,128}. Each world has J independently chosen public causal signs x_j and K-J nuisance coordinates, all deterministic signed copies of a single independent nuisance sign. Legal prefix actions set every causal sign and then the nuisance group. Uniform mass over the full (J+1)-cube gives 2^(J+1) legally reached root states per fixture. All K public coordinates vary, but nuisance rank is only one. The current menu is identical across all roots; prefix actions are not in the diagnostic observation channel.

At the final choice, two dock options use the actual runtime desirability contrast proportional to product(x_j), with opposite signs for the two options. Each selected reaction leads to one forced finishing action, so H1 reward is zero and H2 has a unique winning first action on every root. The player is supplied the public product rule. Both answer classes are exactly balanced and every root is informative. For every subset of causal coordinates and the nuisance proxy, if any causal sign is missing, flip it while holding every observed coordinate fixed. This pairs equal-prior opposite answers, giving exact optimum 1/2. If all causal signs are observed, their product determines the correct dock; optimum 1. All nuisance observations are functions of the same independent nuisance bit, so no subset of padding can replace missing causal information. Causal degree J is independently recovered from the Walsh transform of the actual reaction-score contrast and the binary utility function. It is a deliberately constructed information lower bound, not a universal complexity lower bound for inference.
