# Capacity-sensitive leakage: an exact finite certificate

Draft 0.1 — 2026-10-26. Extends [leak topology](leak_topology.md) beyond rational fibre dimension to finite item capacities, which determine whether two *actual binary records* can collide. Pure exact arithmetic; no model, judge or grader was run. Module: src/spiral_ln/evaluation_leak_capacity.py. Independent tests: tests/test_evaluation_leak_capacity.py. Verifier: scripts/verify_evaluation_leak_capacity.py. Receipt: output/evaluation_leak_capacity/leak_capacity_v0_1.json.

## Setting

Let P be a nonempty support of joint category cells, with sizes \(s_c\ge1\), and let \(M\) contain one 0/1 row for every observed margin. Each feedback face names category maps that are observed jointly; the listed faces are observed together. A record gives cell counts \(0\le x_c\le s_c\). The reported observation is \(Mx\), while the maximally joint channel reveals \(x\).

**Theorem 1 (capacity criterion).** Two distinct feasible cell-count vectors have the same reported observation if and only if

\[
\exists z\in\ker_{\mathbb Z} M\setminus\{0\}\quad\text{such that}\quad |z_c|\le s_c\;\text{for every }c.
\]

*Proof.* Given confused \(x,y\), their difference \(z=x-y\) lies in the integer kernel and obeys the box bound. Conversely, for a bounded kernel vector take \(x_c=\max(z_c,0)\) and \(y_c=\max(-z_c,0)\). Both vectors are feasible, distinct and have the same margins. Every feasible cell-count vector is realized by an underlying binary record. \(\square\)

**Evidence: algebraic identity.** The witness routine returns both count vectors and verifies their margins. Strictness is monotone under componentwise increases in cell capacity. This settles the finite-size question left by leak topology Proposition 10: a positive rational fibre dimension can coexist with an item-level observation at unit capacity.

**Theorem 2 (exact collision law).** Suppose item outcomes are independent fair bits. For independent cell-count tables \(X,X'\),

\[
\Pr[MX=MX']=4^{-\sum_c s_c}
\sum_{\substack{z\in\ker_{\mathbb Z}M\\|z_c|\le s_c}}
\prod_c\binom{2s_c}{s_c+z_c},
\qquad
\Pr[X=X']=4^{-\sum_c s_c}\prod_c\binom{2s_c}{s_c}.
\]

*Proof.* For one cell, Vandermonde's identity gives \(\Pr[X_c-X'_c=z]=4^{-s_c}\binom{2s_c}{s_c+z}\), for \(|z|\le s_c\). Independence factors the probabilities, and agreement on margins is exactly \(M(X-X')=0\). The zero difference is the joint-cell collision. \(\square\)

**Evidence: algebraic identity.** Every feasible difference contributes positive weight. Consequently, the collision ratio is strictly greater than one precisely when Theorem 1 finds a nonzero bounded integer-kernel vector. The implementation enumerates one move in each sign pair using suffix-balance pruning. A separate oracle groups feasible cell-count tables by their margins, weights each by its binomial multiplicity and sums squared group masses. Both algorithms agree on 772 exact, deterministic comparisons.

**Proposition 3 (finite sufficient capacity).** If \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}M=r<n=|P|\), there is a nonzero primitive integer kernel vector supported on at most \(r+1\) cells. For \(r\ge1\) it can be chosen with

\[
\|z\|_\infty \le \max_B|\det B|\le r^{r/2},
\]

where \(B\) ranges over \(r\times r\) submatrices of \(M\). If \(r=0\), use a coordinate unit vector. Uniform capacity at least this bound therefore guarantees strict leakage when the rational kernel is nonzero.

*Proof.* Choose \(r\) independent rows, \(r\) independent columns and one extra column. Signed maximal minors of the resulting \(r\times(r+1)\) matrix give an integral kernel vector. Since the selected rows span the original row space, the vector lies in the full kernel. Divide common factors to make it primitive. Each binary row of an \(r\times r\) minor has Euclidean norm at most \(\sqrt r\), so Hadamard bounds its determinant by \(r^{r/2}\). \(\square\)

**Evidence: algebraic identity.** The exact RREF implementation supplies a primitive certificate with at most \(r+1\) nonzero entries; its maximum absolute entry is a finite sufficient capacity. This bound need not be the smallest feasible capacity.

## Exact five-cell witness

With three maps observed separately, use the ordered support \((000),(001),(011),(101),(110)\). Its integer kernel is generated by

\[
z=(1,-2,1,1,-1).
\]

At unit capacity the separate observation determines every feasible cell-count vector, so the collision ratio is exactly \(1\). Double only the capacity of cell \((001)\), giving \(s=(1,2,1,1,1)\). The bounded nonzero moves are precisely \(z,-z\), and their exact contribution is

\[
\frac{\Pr[MX=MX']}{\Pr[X=X']}=1+2(1/2)^4(1/6)=\boxed{49/48}.
\]

The joint collision probability is \(3/128\), and the separate collision probability is \(49/2048\). Both match independent direct enumeration. The probabilities concern this declared toy prior and make no claim about a real model.

## Exhaustive finite check and boundaries

There are 193 coordinate-surjective supports in the \(2\times2\times2\) grid. Three maps observed separately have 99 supports with positive rational kernel. At unit cell capacity, 91 yield actual collisions and eight remain item-level despite positive rational dimension. Each of those eight admits a witness at uniform capacity two.

The verifier independently checks both collision algorithms for each support and for all three choices of two maps observed jointly with the remaining map separately: 772 comparisons. It records a SHA-256 fingerprint of the ordered cases along with its source hashes. For the pair-joint/third-separate pattern, the matrix is a bipartite graph incidence matrix. Its nonzero cycles admit 0/±1 vectors, so rational positive dimension implies leakage even with unit capacities. This last implication is an algebraic identity; the grid counts are finite verified examples.

The bounded-move search is exponential in the number of cells and capacities. The receipt is a reproducible finite mathematical certificate. No Lean theorem is claimed for this addition. Unequal independent item probabilities require the corresponding exact cell-difference distributions and remain outside its tested scope.
