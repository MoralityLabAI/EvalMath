# VAD-03B / VAD-04A execution ledger

2026-11-25. Evidence: exact synthetic Boolean-reference cost experiments, with an optional learned GRU policy. The finite oracle is not a Lean kernel, and these are not general theorem-proving results.

## VAD-03B: robust signature versus learned bounded search

The local policy was trained on 288 families (1,440 imitation examples generated using training-only auxiliary roots), calibrated against 12 fixed symbolic methods on 144 further families, then tested on 288 families in nine regimes. Clean/shifted cells intentionally share seeds within the test split; seed namespaces across train/calibration/test are disjoint. Test seeds were fixed in code, the checkpoint and calibrated symbolic choice were written before test evaluation, and no independent third party sealed the split. The fixed comparator selected on calibration was q=6 of 8 observations. Eight pair evaluations maximum, common singleton sweep, actual charged index and scoring operations. The learned policy has 545 parameters, 25 epochs, seed 4431; training accuracy 0.4854167. It chooses at most two thresholds or stop from sequential frequency histograms and development score feedback. No planted labels or held-out outcomes enter the policy; auxiliary training rewards are computed on training-only roots.

Pooled baseline 4,718,592 units. q6 cost 4,321,291 (1.091940x); GRU 4,969,088 (0.949589x), with 1,028,448 additional neural MACs (0.786755x when one MAC costs one abstract unit). Original strict signature 4,716,392 (1.000466x); memoryless 4,976,172 (0.948237x); shuffled 5,027,467 (0.938563x). Excluding its 645,120 charged feature units, a descriptive sensitivity gives GRU 1.091264x, still below q6 before MAC costs. Training cost is separately logged (2.579s for this run), not amortized into deployment cost. The learned arm fails the advancement gate; no TRM superiority or persistent-library advantage is established. Retraining reproduced checkpoint hash and every reported record exactly in the local environment. This is a tiny GRU, not the full TRM architecture.

At 25% corruption strict recovered 3/32, q6 30/32 and GRU 30/32. At 50%, strict recovered 4/32, q6 30/32 and GRU 28/32. Gains for q6 / GRU: clean 1.191/1.014, noise2 1.180/1.018, noise4 1.177/1.000, structured noise 1.128/0.984, shifted 0.924/0.809. Regime-wise paired bootstrap intervals for symbolic-cost-minus-learned-cost are all below zero; these are descriptive, not multiplicity-adjusted inferential claims.

## VAD-04A: candidate invention

Remove the supplied pool for all invention arms. Dimensions 36, target weights 9/10/11, motif widths 3/4/5/6, eight observed roots; seven regimes, 168 calibration and 336 test families. Independent calibration picked pairwise intersections without a validation veto. Each test family has 1,024 held-out deployment roots and 64 separately declared validation roots. Calibration/test namespaces are disjoint. A privileged comparator alone sees a supplied complementary pair. Intersection search enumerates at most 28 pairwise intersections and selects a useful single cache by projected development saving less construction charge. Other arms include coordinate frequencies, ranked prefixes, hybrid proposals and two-cache search. No invented candidate has access to the planted pair. All exact target costs are computed using the earlier XOR cost model.

Pooled baseline 7,315,712. Intersection invention cost 6,101,081 (1.199085x), privileged-pair comparator 6,225,642 (1.175094x). Net profitable in 238/240 constructive families; 0/96 null/shifted. Regime gains intersection / privileged: clean 1.415269/1.291227; noise2 1.403358/1.298847; noise4 1.317036/1.301233; structured 1.349614/1.327475; multi 1.119051/1.121881; null 0.984445/0.998391; shifted 0.985019/0.995968. The direct single cache removes artificial dependence on the supplied complementary pair. In a fixed motif M with independent uniform size-r nuisance supports chosen from n coordinates, two clean roots intersect exactly in M with probability choose(n-r,r)/choose(n,r), for 2r <= n. This is a combinatorial explanation, not a generalization bound for the without-replacement mixed generator.

Independent validation: score the frozen candidate on 64 separate roots proved with primitives, charge discovery and probe costs even on veto, and evaluate 1,024 untouched roots. Empirical net-saving veto selected 236/336 candidates and rejected all 96 null/shifted families; pooled cost 6,227,049 (1.174828x). Margin variant selected 154/336, cost 6,401,329 (1.142843x). The margin is Hoeffding-inspired, not a formal confidence guarantee for the mixed without-replacement generator. Abstention cannot recover sunk discovery work. Under this mixture independent validation improves selection integrity at a net cost.

## Verification and decision

Five local tests pass: robust index vs direct filter; disjoint seeds and query caps; no planted access; frozen receipt and exact-cost verification; full invention replay. Full GRU retraining also matched the checkpoint and all 4,320 records. Invention has 7,056 records. Canonical record SHA256 hashes (JSON sorted keys, compact separators) and source/checkpoint digests are in the compact receipt. The optional torch driver is isolated from normal exact-math tests. The full receipts/checkpoint are delivered separately in the experiment bundle.

The gate stops neural escalation. Promote the intersection and q6 symbolic controls as baselines for a new gym with actual Lean statement fidelity, legal premise environments, proof verification and measured kernel/model costs. A future model must win against invention-enabled baselines, rather than solve a supplied-pool task where direct composition has been artificially excluded. Lean formalization and real neural theorem proving remain outstanding.
