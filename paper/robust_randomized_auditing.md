# NFAS-03: Randomized robust auditing and the order filtration
Research draft, 2026-11-25. Extends NFAS-02. Exact finite certificates, paper proofs, no Lean certificate.

## Scope
Same mandatory perfect audit of one of two candidates, both provisionally authorized, unaudited candidate remains authorized. U = verified utility V_i(1-X_i); R = unaudited failure X_(1-i). Failure bits and utility bits independent fair; noisy feedback from independent flips. At the first arrival observe its identity, its noisy bit and its value, then choose audit first or reserve audit for the second. Each of eight possible observations gets an action-1 probability p_j (reserve). Utility pairs (1,3),(1,3) and (1,5),(2,6); noise 0,1/4,1/2; lambda=1,2,4,8. All 48 LPs on this declared exploratory grid are solved with rational arithmetic. No real model or judge runs.

## Theorem 3: exact robust randomized support
Let F,S be the failure-first and success-first kernels. Independent order is their equal mixture, including fair tie-breaking. Therefore the worst scalar payoff across the three occurs at F or S. A randomized decision table p in [0,1]^8 is sufficient for one decision, and U_k(p),R_k(p) are affine. The scalar robust value is
    max_p min_k [U_k(p)-lambda R_k(p)]
  = min_{theta in [0,1]} max_p theta*(U_F-lambda R_F)+(1-theta)*(U_S-lambda R_S).
The rectangular robust value is instead
    max_p [min_k U_k(p)-lambda max_k R_k(p)]
  = min_{theta,phi in [0,1]} max_p
      theta U_F+(1-theta)U_S-lambda*(phi R_F+(1-phi)R_S).
Both follow from finite-dimensional bilinear minimax (equivalently LP duality). The inner maximum separates over eight observation cells. Its dual is convex piecewise affine, minimized at an endpoint/intersection of the lines where an action changes. In the scalar case theta=phi. In the rectangular case they are independent. The verifier enumerates these vertices exactly, identifies primal ties, searches the box intersected by the two complementary-slackness conditions (at most two fractional coordinates at a vertex), and checks exact equality of primal and dual objectives. Every certificate stores both multipliers and the action probabilities. Independently enumerates all 256 deterministic tables for each objective to quantify any randomization gain. Independent order is included by the convex-mixture identity. Rectangular objective need not equal the scalar objective; NFAS-02's robust rectangle corresponds to this second LP.

## Theorem 4: private seed coupling for fully informed free ordering
The scheduler observes the entire world X,Y,V and public independent samples S, and can choose either order. The private seed model gives the scheduler the randomized policy but not its draw; public seed additionally reveals that draw before the order. Fix a scalar payoff g_i = V_i(1-X_i)-lambda X_(1-i) for auditing identity i. For a fixed world write p,q for probabilities of auditing identity 0 when it arrives first or second. Worst order private payoff is
    min(p*g_0+(1-p)*g_1, q*g_0+(1-q)*g_1).
Use a common uniform threshold Z for the identity-0 decisions under both orders. The events Z<=p and Z<=q are nested, so this expression equals the expectation over Z of the pointwise minimum payoff of the two deterministic actions. The threshold policy is globally implementable: for each first observation define its probability of auditing identity 0 and couple all cells with the same threshold. Interchange finite world expectation and threshold integration. Thus the private-seed worst-case expectation is an average of deterministic worst-case expectations, bounded by the best deterministic policy. That policy is also feasible with a public seed. The optimal scalar values coincide. Public independent samples cannot improve the known-law optimum since one can optimize each sample-conditioned policy separately under the identical world law. The result relies on exactly two audit identities, a scalar payoff, unrestricted order choice by a fully informed adversary and one decision. It does not apply automatically to a partially informed adversary, mixed-kernel commitment games or rectangular risk constraints.

The oracle exhausts deterministic tables and checks the coupling identity for 64 fractional tables at each of the 24 instance/price pairs. This verifies the toy theorem independently at the declared finite points, not a general prophet inequality.

## Independent calibration sample control
Each candidate gets an independent public sample from its two-point value distribution. The simple threshold policy audits first if the first value strictly exceeds the maximum sample, otherwise reserves the mandatory audit for the second. This is a quota-enforcing toy policy, not the OpenAI algorithm. Exact evaluation compares independent order, order chosen as a function of the value vector and sample vector only (seed immaterial for the deterministic rule), and order chosen with full knowledge of X,Y,V,S. The last scheduler has strictly more information than the one in the prophet manuscript. A fixed-identity audit is a control. All probabilities are integer weighted over the complete world grid; the values-and-samples scheduler minimizes conditional expected scalar payoff before the hidden failures/noise are realized. No claim that either scheduler represents an empirical setting. The known-law optimum for a partially informed private-seed scheduler remains open.
