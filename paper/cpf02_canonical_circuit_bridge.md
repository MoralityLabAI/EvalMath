# CPF-02: pinned circuit conventions and canonical claim obligations

Status: stacked research draft. Algebraic simulation proof, bounded exact formal-polynomial census, and candidate Lean statement bridges. Upstream proof rebuild and full class equivalence remain open. No error rates are inferred from these papers.

## Circuit-level result

Fix a field and finite variable type. In the OpenAI comparator, an exact s-gate table uses free variables and field scalars; each gate adds, subtracts, or multiplies two available values. Anthropic's charged table has addition/multiplication gates and explicit variable/constant gates. The output can be any earlier gate. Our canonical natural-index table has free leaves and subtraction. Its family predicates bound arity, degree and circuit size eventually polynomially; the witness size is a polynomially bounded natural number and the VNP sum ranges over Boolean assignments to that many variables. The approximation predicate uses Laurent coefficients, without claiming equivalence to Zariski closure.

For charged-to-free simulation preserve the indices of the entire charged table; a charged leaf is computed by adding zero to the free leaf. Earlier operation references remain legal. Hence every charged witness of size s gives a free witness of size s. For free-to-charged, insert one constant -1; translate the two operands of every operation, inserting at most two charged leaf gates. Addition and multiplication need one further gate; subtraction uses multiplication by -1 and addition. Store the correspondence between original and translated gate indices. For a free output leaf insert one additional leaf; a gate output uses its corresponding translated index. The result has at most 4s+2 gates and exactly the same formal polynomial. Pad with constants to obtain exactly 4s+2 if needed. This proof is valid in all characteristics, including two; it never uses evaluations at field points to identify polynomials. For minimum gate counts C_O and C_A, C_O <= C_A <= 4C_O+2. Polynomial and superpolynomial exact size therefore agree under these gate models on a common sequence of polynomials.

## Verification scopes

The Lean module imports the pinned Anthropic statement and the definitions-only extraction of the OpenAI comparator, so the candidate bridges are expressed against the actual upstream definition bodies. The comparator's admitted challenge endpoint stays in an archive outside the build. Candidate lemmas: OpenAI exact Computes iff canonical Computes, Anthropic charged Computes implies canonical Computes at equal budget, Anthropic Laurent approximation implies canonical approximation at equal budget, and an exact lower-bound transfer in that direction. The reverse 4s+2 compiler proof is presently a paper argument supported by a finite formal-polynomial census, not a Lean theorem. CI must pass before attributing even the four candidate statements to Lean.

The finite census covers zero, one, and two original arithmetic gates, all topologically valid operand choices over two variables and constants -1,0 plus earlier gate references, every legal output, and fields of orders 2,3,5. It compares sparse formal monomial coefficient maps, not truth tables. The two implementations share polynomial arithmetic; this is a compiler semantics test, not an independent arithmetic oracle. The first implementation exposed an output-leaf bug (the table was snapshotted before appending the output); it was fixed before the committed census.

## Remaining obligations and evidence boundaries

* Normalize the asymptotic polynomial bound to Anthropic's all-index n^a+a, with finite-prefix padding and n=0,1 handled explicitly; prove the bound both ways.
* Prove that arbitrary finite witness types in OpenAI's VNP can be transported to Fin of their cardinality, that the summation is invariant under this equivalence, and that witness size and degree bounds agree. Resolve the restricted Fin n × Bool class endpoint versus the all-families proper-subset endpoint. VP inclusion in VNP also needs its own proof.
* Kernel-check the reverse compiler on arbitrary gate tables, and the exact-to-constant-Laurent embedding, then decide which Laurent notion agrees with the geometric border notion over the stated field classes. Do not infer geometric closure from a Laurent statement alone.
* Match the Hamiltonian/permanent definitions, orientation, index and characteristic, and compare quantitative bounds only after matching the underlying families, scales, and quantifiers. OpenAI's comparator endpoint is exact separation; its documentation gives additional exact lower-bound scope but is not a theorem signature for a quantitative border bound. Anthropic's statement file explicitly contains quantitative exact and approximate endpoints. Neither upstream solution is imported or claimed verified in this branch.

For CPF-03 use controlled mutants: gate cost mismatch, omitted degree bound, nonuniform field threshold, characteristic-two permanent claim, exact-for-border substitution, and finite evaluation mistaken for polynomial equality. Assign labels by a proved implication or a countermodel, with unresolved cases segregated; blind the model to label and proof provenance. This is a prospective test design, not measured model accuracy.
