## 1. State geometry

Let x=(actual BNumbers, observer-owned pValues, observer-owned p2Values, discrete encounter/spool/commitment state). Keep owner, subject, property, time and epistemic order explicit; do not hash them in gold tables. Let S be the reachable state set, A(x) the legal player menu, O_i the pre-decision observation, T(x,a) the actual successor law, and u the independently declared utility/ending certificate. Distinguish authored NPC reaction selection from the player's optimization. Freeze both.

On normalized numerical coordinates, HG-01 supplies the Clifford operator D_w(x)=sum_j w_j x_j Gamma_j, with anticommutator 2 delta_ij I. Thus ||D_w(x)-D_w(y)||_op^2 = sum_j w_j^2 (x_j-y_j)^2. Use positive rational weights w_j (amplitudes); zero weights create a pseudometric. Choose scales and weights before seeing the test results. Discrete coordinates may use the factorized Hilbert kernel (9/25)^Hamming; the normalized chordal squared distance is 2(1-kernel). Neither metric implies that geometric proximity predicts an optimal action. A secret gate can use a radius or joint region around a declared ending prototype g. Existing Sweepweave "Dirac operators" are transition scripts, not literal Clifford operators. A metric and a transition must be tested separately.

For a declared deterministic realized path x_0,...,x_T and fixed goal g, define E_t=d(x_t,x_{t+1})^2, P_t=d(x_t,g)^2-d(x_{t+1},g)^2.
Identity: sum_t P_t=d(x_0,g)^2-d(x_T,g)^2 by telescoping. E_t is nonnegative, P_t signed. Report max(E)/sum(E), max(P_positive)/sum(P_positive), zero conventions, per-act distributions and norm by event type. Do not confuse a share of squared path-step energy with share of net displacement. For multiple goals or changing metrics retain a separate ledger. The nudge/Set/Blend engine semantics must be evaluated on reachable states (including saturation and ordered writes), not inferred from AST operator names.

Define goal slack for a ball gate m_g(x)=r^2-d(x,g)^2. Gate crossing is a sign change under a legal transition, with equality/ties specified. For non-ball gates report the actual predicate margin where meaningful. Large shifts near a gate can be justified by disclosed actions and prior preparation. Track goal-directed progress and nuisance movement separately, and use actual terminal utility/counterfactual effects as the adjudicator.

## 2. Decision-relevant formula audit

For encounter e, action a and legal NPC reaction r, the frozen formula f_r(x) maps a feasible state to a desirability score. In the deterministic argmax reference, R(x)=argmax_r f_r(x), a set; ties must not acquire unregistered semantics. If the live engine samples reactions, audit its actual normalized probability law and compare distributions instead of using the argmax reference.

For coordinate j, compare reachable or independently justified do-interventions (x,x') with other coordinates fixed. Record counts of feasible pairs, changes in f_r-f_s, changes in winner set, and downstream utility difference after the complete effect and consequence. Relative score influence distinguishes common-mode terms from consequential ones. A coordinate with zero feasible pairs is unidentified. A coordinate with no single-coordinate effect on sampled support may matter jointly; test paired and higher-order interventions on feasible joint support, not arbitrary inconsistent p/p2 assignments. For Boolean full cubes use the Mobius coefficients of score contrasts and terminal payoff; interaction degree is the largest order with a nonzero coefficient. On constrained domains mark degree as representation/support dependent. Report sign, magnitude, saturation, thresholds, units, prior narrative warrant and provenance of each influential variable.

Compare a proposed reaction with a minimal semantically plausible formula, a coefficient/sign mutant, a matched-sham variable replacement, and a common-mode/no-op mutant. Match AST count, transition structure and observable length where feasible. A fit to observed reactions alone may leave causal structure unidentifiable; test held-out interventions and reject an unsupported unique-formula claim. In stochastic worlds add total variation of reaction kernels and downstream value perturbations. Do not reward formula complexity without measured benefit. Real effects are audited via replay, counterfactual utility differences, and gate crossing; causal credit over dependent transitions requires a declared intervention and continuation policy.

## 3. Reasoning demand and anti-deus-ex-machina certificate

Static finite reference: hidden state s drawn from rational prior p; initial observation o(s); registered deterministic query labels q_j(s); one terminal action with common menu and utilities u(s,a). All queries available before commitment, with unit cost; no new state transitions. For a cell B of positive-prior states use *unnormalized* W(B)=max_a sum_{s in B} p(s)u(s,a). Let
    V_0(B,Q)=W(B)
    V_b(B,Q)=max(W(B), max_{q in Q} sum_{C in split_q(B)} V_{b-1}(C,Q\{q}))
and sum over initial observation cells. This is the exact finite optimal adaptive value by conditioning on the first query and induction. V_full=sum_s p(s)max_a u(s,a). V_0 <= V_b <= V_all <= V_full. Fixed-set queries use one partition instead; adaptive value dominates a best fixed set at the same budget.

Define inaccessible gap G=V_full-V_all; recoverable demand R=V_all-V_0; budget b_epsilon=min{b: V_b >= V_all-epsilon}; full-information budget is undefined when G>0. Large G under the declared actor filtration means success requires unavailable information *relative to the full-state objective*, not evidence of reasoning difficulty. If ambiguity is intentional, grade posterior-optimal safe decisions, value of information and calibrated abstention; retain full-state value only as a diagnostic. For any observation cell with two indistinguishable states requiring different unique optimal actions, positive mass and positive margin imply positive regret for every policy on that cell. Exact bound follows by maximizing common action over the cell. If hidden worlds have equal priors and opposite unique binary success actions, V=1/2, V_full=1. This is a mathematical witness of an unfair deterministic secret gate when the evaluator expects full-information success. It does not outlaw chance or tragedy with an appropriate utility contract.

Sequential extension (not implemented in SRG-01): finite state, histories, pre-decision observation kernels and legal menus. For belief b and horizon h use belief-state Bellman recursion, integrating T, O and observation-contingent future policies. Declare affordances, costs, irreversible actions, stochastic seed and every post-decision reveal. An observation quotient must preserve available continuations and utility; a one-step winner signature does not establish continuation sufficiency. Test the objective oracle, permitted-information oracle, local greedy, random legal and heuristic baselines on matched roots. Secret endpoints need verified feasible paths, meaningful prior cues and no unauthorized late rules. The author must supply an evidence provenance ledger for every decisive variable and rule at each irreversible action. Surprise can be legitimate through a low-prior event or inferable hidden state; arbitrary evaluator-only knowledge, unreachable gates, retroactive rule changes, accidental shortcuts and unannounced disproportionate effects are separately flagged. Positive surprise is not a penalty by itself.

## 4. Difficulty and K

The existing high-K language concerns many state variables and projections. Use K_ambient for declared variable count, K_latent for independent latent coordinates (when defined), K_relevant for causal support/rank under a frozen task, K_joint for interaction width, and L_belief for epistemic nesting. Keep query budget b, horizon H, branch factor, noise/entropy and computational search budget distinct. TRM controller k_budget and HG-05 goal rank are separately named quantities. An exact minimal sufficient subset or quotient on a small finite model is stronger evidence than a raw count; for general nonlinear tasks report a feasible upper/lower bound, not an asserted rank. An invertible coordinate reparameterization can change sparsity; register the semantic basis.

Example: a uniformly random independent K-bit parity objective, coordinate queries only. Until all K relevant coordinates are known, one unknown fair bit flips the parity while leaving history unchanged; conditional success is 1/2. At K, success is 1. This holds even for adaptive queries, with any number of independent nuisance bits. If one relevant coordinate has no permitted query, V_all=1/2 and G=1/2. This is an analytic identity for a declared synthetic access model, not an empirical scaling claim. Parity is a control, not a narrative-generation template. With target chosen by a first hidden selector and two possible data bits, two adaptive queries achieve 1 versus 3/4 for best two fixed queries. Distinguish information acquisition from subsequent computational reasoning.

Proposed factorial pilot (not yet registered as sealed):
- Keep existing tier 1/2/3/4/5 horizon targets 20/32/80/135/300 and their intended capabilities; begin with compressed 4-12-turn witnesses, then replay native worlds.
- K_ambient = 8, 32, 140, 512, 1024; K_relevant strata = 2, 4, 8, 16 where feasible; matched padding and native-active increases, holding topology and clues stable for padding. Record actual p/p2 support, semantic variable basis, cross-agent coupling, formula degree and latent channel entropy.
- For each tier use evidence-aware local choice, memory accumulation, multi-constraint planning, belief revision/deception, and nested adaptive causal repair as distinct task families; vary L_belief=0,1,2 independently when relevant. Higher orders require explicitly implemented epistemic semantics.
- Run paired clean, common-mode, sham effect, saturation, late windfall, invisible gate, redundant clue, recoverable deception, and order-permutation variants; balance effects by event class, vary clue access and sizes independently. Maintain matched text/structure and seeded randomization. Separate author and player splits, hide oracle labels and patches from the player, group confidence intervals by independent world. Freeze difficulty targets, margins, declared utility, simulator and scoring before held-out play.
- For each world report ending distribution by policy, attainable utility, unavoidable versus avoidable regret, b_epsilon, forecast calibration, conditional causal support and effect profiles, successful-plan length, choice margin and search-node expansion. Measure model tokens, steps and elapsed time separately; do not translate oracle query counts into neural FLOPs. Random-play ending diversity is descriptive; use author-declared reachability/rarity targets and confidence bounds. A large last-act effect is permitted if warranted by accumulated state and evidence, not by unexplained rescue.

## 5. Authoring problem

Choose a world W to minimize deviation from a declared difficulty/ending profile subject to semantic and causal gates: observed evidence before commitments, legal reachability, frozen utility and engine semantics, interpretable variable/effect provenance, meaningful intervention response, and registered safety constraints. Do not use a single scalar that can trade an inaccessible required ending against more AST nodes. Use a feasible set and a Pareto profile over (recoverable demand, budget curve, effect concentration, reaction support, goal diversity, author cost). Declare which variables are intended core versus intentional distractors. Audit the author on blinded repair tasks: fix a sign, choose an appropriate input, resize an effect, add a legitimate clue, or prove a constraint unsatisfiable. Evaluate the player separately on the repaired instance. Inverse authoring asks whether compatible mechanisms can be distinguished from traces; return equivalence sets where support is insufficient.
