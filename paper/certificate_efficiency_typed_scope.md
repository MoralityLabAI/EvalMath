# ZKL-18: cost-profile boundary for dependent typing

The ZKL-17 Nova conversion circuit measures explicit de Bruijn lifting, substitution and beta conversion. Its hosted run (https://github.com/patrickdugan/zk-rusto-no-bitcoino/actions/runs/45580689921/job/139089678486) measured 16,305 raw constraints per fixed step and verified 34 honest rows across five fixtures. All five compressed proofs serialized to 11,128 bytes. Those figures apply to that explicit raw-term relation.

ZKL-18 (source a5c6acb571138342fb0090ef1303b46645b2ae87) uses public syntax and a trusted native semantic compiler, then authenticates typed judgments as a finite local inference table. The native compiler checks capture-avoiding substitution and beta normalization outside Nova. Report three distinct quantities: source/native semantic admission work, raw step constraints times verified derivation rows, and actual setup/folding/compression/verification timing. Do not call fewer raw constraints a cheaper equivalent verifier when work has moved outside the circuit. Cost identity must bind the statement grammar, semantics checked inside and outside the circuit, state shape and backend revision.

For the public table, define F_0=empty, F_(n+1)=F_n union {h(r): P(r) subset F_n}. The least fixed point is exactly the judgments admitting finite derivations over the table: induction on iterations constructs finite derivations; induction on a derivation proves inclusion at a finite iteration. This is a simple algebraic/set-theoretic property conditional on valid rule admission, not a claim about arbitrary Lean. It provides a native baseline with no private witness. If goal-reachable rules have unique producers and acyclic dependencies, the cardinality of the goal prerequisite closure is the minimum distinct-judgment proof size: each prerequisite is necessary by unique production, and topological ordering constructs a proof of that size. This minimum does not extend to other rule tables or unrestricted dependent calculus.
