# SRG-AC01: Automatic authoring certificate and scoped causal witnesses

2026-11-25. Public exploratory synthetic and sampled structural analysis using actual compiled SWMD-SRG/2. Additive module after the concurrent SRG-20 registration; preserves SRG-01–20 and the frozen original and SRG-13 Reservoir Dogs fixtures. No paid compute, model inference, independent private validation, Lean or production editor modifications. Source-level execution in isolated ECMAScript, not standalone Node CLI.

## 1. Three evidence scopes

An authored reaction formula may mention a variable without using it in a meaningful contrast. The certificate first inventories typed AST pointers in encounter availability, wild priority, option visibility/performability, reaction gates, desirability and effects. For ordered writes it records last-writer-to-later-reader edges inside each reaction, including spool metadata. Static unread means no pointer references in the compiled formulas; it is not proof of narrative irrelevance, because prose, external UI and nonformula semantics may matter. Static dependencies are possible dependencies, not causal results.

Given two states, audit all legal actions using the existing runtime. Compare menu, eligible reaction scores, eligibility, pairwise eligible score differences, complete winner sets, selected reaction, destination or destination-admission flag, change in numerical state relative to the input, and successor spools. Compare the full root-action vectors Q_H(s,a)=1 if a legal continuation reaches the declared terminal goal within H moves, zero otherwise, for H=1 and 3. These are existential full-state values; they do not certify what an information-constrained player can know. A change in Q vector can arise from a change in action menu; record the menu separately. Score/contrast/effect numerical comparisons use absolute tolerance 1e-10; selected reactions and routes use actual deterministic runtime semantics. Counts are per probe (any legal action changes), not per changed action. The score flag includes eligibility changes. Route flag includes changed admission even if the explicit-mode route stays fixed.

There are two distinct counterfactual evidence scopes:
- Exact reachable state matches preserve page, turn, spools, seen and visited arrays and all coordinates except one. A witness can be stronger than a free-coordinate intervention. No pair is no identification, never evidence of zero causal support.
- A local structural do probe changes one numeric coordinate by +/-.125 with clipping to [-1,1], holding all other numeric and discrete fields fixed. Rerun actual reaction and continuation code. Such a state can violate correlations or provenance established by the prefix. The evidence is causal sensitivity within the supplied structural simulator, not a reachable-history causal effect. Exceptions (including any invalid selected route during the bounded continuation) are counted and excluded. The current implementation does not retain exception types; those need follow-up classification. Each first positive witness retains root and history/action identifiers, the intervention and before/after chosen-reaction and Q vectors; rerun the pinned source to inspect exact scores or effects. No automated accept/reject on story quality.

## 2. Controlled legal Boolean cube

For J in {1,3,5}, declared signal/nuisance K in {8,32}, all-public vs first-causal-bit-hidden, create 12 genuine SWMD-SRG/2 fixtures. There are J causal bits, a common-mode score bit c, an effect-only bit e, K-J-2 constant padding coordinates and one hidden output ledger (total declared coordinates K+1). Reach every point of the n=J+2 dimensional cube via legal prefix options; each sets one signed BNumber to +/-1. All roots have identical encounter, turn and active spool. Their histories differ, but the entire decision suffix has no metadata pointers, uses explicit direct strict routing to fixed suffix destinations, and does not depend on past events. The complete suffix never revisits the prefix. Thus varying one coordinate has a controlled feasible paired contrast under the fixed suffix; this is separate from the stronger full-discrete-state match in Section 1.

The correct route sign is product of J causal signs. Each dock has a winning and losing candidate reaction with desirability +/- .25 times dock-sign times product, plus .125*c common to both. A numeric effect writes .125*e to the output ledger under either reaction. A success/fail branch then requires one further legal action to reach its corresponding ending; the goal is the successful ending. No utility depends on the effect ledger. The common bit changes raw scores without changing their difference, winner or outcome. The effect bit changes the numerical successor without changing the scores, reaction or goal outcome. Padding is syntactically unread and constant in the tested cube; no claim about a freely varied full K-dimensional cube.

Use Walsh coefficients fhat(S)=2^-n sum_x f(x) product_{j in S}x_j for the score contrast and Q_3(dock_pos). The full signed cube has highest nonzero degree J for both; e and c cancel. H1 action values all zero, H3 has one unique winning action per root (the actual minimum success horizon here is 2). 672 legally reached roots and 2160 cube-edge comparisons passed. Changing K from 8 to 32 did not change degree. Current observation alone has utility ceiling 1 if all bits visible and 1/2 if the first is hidden (uniform mass over legal prefix choices). Including the visible prefix actions restores ceiling 1 because the action IDs disclose the assigned bit. The continuation after the root is forced, so grouping by current observation or full history suffices to compute the exact corresponding policy ceiling in this fixture. This is a memory/filtration witness; the hidden coordinate is not inherently unknowable.

## 4. Geometry and effect apportionment

The receipt records per-prefix cumulative raw squared-step energy, maximum step energy, and maximum root selected-branch energy. Maximum prefix step share max_t E_t/sum_t E_t across sampled roots: original .166626, candidate .185991. Largest prefix step energy .041772 and .061700; largest selected root-branch energy .046156 and .047912. These are raw unit-weight squared BNumber shifts in realized prefixes, not a validated normative cap or a goal-directed metric. The candidate includes four additional coordinates, so total raw energies are not dimension-matched. A defensible authoring certificate must require predeclared semantic/epistemic/utility blocks, scale weights, a provenance ledger and registered tolerances. Distinguish numeric changes in bookkeeping from causal state movement and actual payoff. Large movement or high concentration is a review flag requiring narrative warrant; a small move can still cross a decisive threshold. The current certificate deliberately reports measurements rather than automatically rejecting a narrative for exceeding an invented threshold.

## 5. Authoring workflow and follow-up

The runtime-derived inventory and bounded witnesses can be consumed by the TypeScript editor: identify referenced variables, distinguish score movement from relative desirability and actual selected reactions, surface ordered write dependencies, and inspect reachable or intervention-based witnesses. Use a separate goal and observation protocol for every authored evaluation. Certify a claimed interaction degree only on a declared reachable cube or explicit intervention domain; certify a claimed planning demand with a horizon/action contrast and an observation-constrained oracle. The sample is public and the goal binary; independent private evaluation, calibrated utility weights, formal narrative warrant, robust geometric apportionment and production editor integration remain unverified. SRG-17 and SRG-20 higher-order reasoning axes are complementary, not inferred from counts of p/p2 coordinates. The current CLI audit defaults to the Reservoir Dogs contract; a general editor integration needs user-supplied target encounter and goal, schema and variable-group annotations. Do not use it as a universal authoring quality score.
