# ACD-04: direct global polynomial and target-directed synthesis

Frozen 2026-11-25 before running simulation. Exact finite symbolic pilot,
not neural, Lean, or GPU research. Use fresh seeds and families.

Worlds: dimensions 5,6,7,8; three affine-action families:
(1) hidden-subspace translations, (2) coordinate permutations preserving
Hamming weight, (3) coordinate reset actions (noninvertible). Conjugate
actions by invertible affine coordinate changes. Calibration and test use
different seeds, mixing lengths and swap choices. Six calibration and
twelve test worlds per dimension/family: 216 total. Sixteen distinct
targets per world from a separate seeded shuffle. All states checked
against independent hidden-coordinate orbit closure. No family tag in
the selector.

Define phi(x) as coordinates and all pair products (square-free).
For each action F, the vector D_F(x)=phi(F(x))+phi(x) has degree <=2.
The constraint row span of all D_F(x) is generated by D_F(0),
D_F(e_i), and D_F(e_i+e_j): evaluations at these anchors suffice by
finite differences. A coefficient vector y orthogonal to the rows
defines a globally invariant polynomial y dot phi(x).
For target difference u=phi(s)+phi(t), if u lies outside the row
span, construct a separator y with y dot u=1 and verify y against
the basis or raw anchors. If u lies inside, no globally invariant
polynomial of degree <=2 separates that pair. This does not imply
reachability. The source-specific hull is at least as strong and
may strictly outperform global invariants for noninvertible maps.

Arms: lazy BFS; linear and quadratic source hull (construct induced
affine lift maps, close source displacement basis, audit invariance);
global degree-2 row span (eager anchors, echelon basis, verify target
separator against basis); target-directed degree-2 CEGIS (start with
empty constraint basis, propose separator of target difference, scan
raw anchors and add violating row, retain constraints and validated
polynomials across queries; memoize anchor rows when evaluated).
All unresolved targets fall back to lazy exact BFS. All successful
exclusion certificates checked: source hull invariant audit plus
target nonmembership; global row separator against complete row
span or all raw anchors; CEGIS can reuse a previously verified
polynomial. All BFS path or closed-set certificates independently
checked on original actions. All work including failed candidate
proposals, anchor evaluation, caching, build/audit and certificates
charged. Offline oracle and independent correctness census excluded.
Count selected reads, writes, bitset/algebra and checks at unit weight.
BigInt vector operations count one unit independent of width. No
measured wall time, memory, or bit-complexity claim.

ACD-04A: census global vs source-specific degree-2 exclusions,
global degree-1 exclusions, and strict counterexamples. Independently
audit every global row span against D_F(x) for every Boolean state,
and every reported exclusion against original orbit. Exercise
malformed separator/invalid proof controls.

ACD-04B: compare work and verified prefix closure in matched budgets
16 times 128,512,2048 units per world (512 primary). Budget censoring
retrospective. Calibration chooses minimum aggregate work per public
dimension among fixed arms. Record all test worlds regardless of screen.
ACD-04C: require calibration per-world hindsight headroom >=20% over
dimension-conditioned dispatch in at least one dimension and >=5pp
primary oracle closure headroom on its corresponding test subset.
Only then consider a future sealed neural trial. Also report all-world
diagnostic and comparison to strong symbolic baselines. No retuning.
